Selected work

PAYMENTS · 2025

A ledger that could not disagree with itself

Series-A marketplace, ~£40M annual volume, three separate balance calculations that drifted apart by up to 2% after a failed payout.

The company did not have a money bug. It had three money models that each believed they were the source of truth.

  • Introduced a single double-entry ledger with an append-only journal.
  • Every existing balance became a derived view, with a nightly reconciliation job that fails loudly.
  • Difference between reported and settled balances went from 2.1% to zero in six weeks.
“The audit paid for itself in the first month — it found a rounding path that was silently losing money on every refund.”
